# Maintainers

The people who maintain octo-pod, and the roles each one holds.
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md#roles-and-responsibilities) describes each role, and how
maintainers join and leave.

The roster is published here once each maintainer has agreed to be named in this
public repository. Until then, open an issue to reach the maintainers.

Code owners are the members of the team that [.github/CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS)
names.

To reach the maintainers, open an issue. Report a vulnerability privately, as
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes.
